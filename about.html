About Us

Learn more about our journey, our team, and our passion for food.

Our Journey

Founded in 2020, TruckFleet began as a small idea to connect food lovers with the best local street food. Today, we are a thriving network of over 50 trucks serving delicious meals across the city.

Our mission is simple: To provide accessible, high-quality, and diverse food options to everyone, everywhere.
